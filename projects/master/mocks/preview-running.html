  • The four shipped controls keep their size, order and 12pt spacing inside a fixed 200pt frame, trailing-aligned in the 480pt content column — .frame(width: 200, alignment: .trailing). The panel grows leftward from a pinned trailing edge, so trailing alignment is what keeps the row at the same screen position; with no alignment argument the row centres and slides 152pt left. Nothing in the row reflows or moves when the panel grows (AC-PNL-5). See panel resize.
  • The preview well is 480×270pt with a 6pt corner radius, clipped, and never overlaps the row above it.
  • The control strip sits below the preview: camera picker on the leading edge, mirror checkbox trailing. See the placement decision for why they are not overlaid on the image.
  • The picker's displayed selection is the passive camera-name indicator required by AC-PV-6 — plain label colour, no icon tint, no caution glyph (AC-PV-7).
  • The image is mirrored, the default. Mirroring flips the preview layer only; nothing about Photo Booth changes.

  • Every connected video device, by display name, in discovery order (AC-CAM-1). No "Default" pseudo-entry: the app resolves the default silently and shows the real device name, so the label is always literally true.
  • A checkmark marks the device currently being previewed — which after a fallback is not necessarily the persisted preference (AC-CAM-4).
  • Long names truncate with an ellipsis in the closed control and never widen the panel (AC-CAM-8). The open menu is free to be wider than the control; it is a floating menu, not panel content.
  • The picker is 480pt minus the mirror checkbox and one 12pt gap. At that width "Logitech BRIO 4K Ultra HD Pro Webcam" fits without truncating.

Focus ring: the shipped panel sets .focusEffectDisabled() on the row, and AC-AX-4 requires the preview and its controls to match. The master mocks already flag the invisible focus ring as an open question for a later pass; this feature inherits it rather than diverging from it.

Menu bar and keyboard

Unchanged. The app is still a MenuBarExtra-only agent app with no application menu bar, no Settings scene and no commands {} block, so this feature registers no keyboard shortcut and adds no menu item. The camera picker and the mirror checkbox are reachable by Tab inside the panel and by VoiceOver, and by nothing else. That is a deliberate continuation of the shipped design, not an oversight: adding a Settings scene for two preferences would introduce the first window this app has ever had. If a future pass adds an application menu, the mirror toggle is the one item that would earn a View-menu entry.
