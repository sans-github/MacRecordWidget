Idle

Nothing recording, no preview. Four controls and no text: pin, audio, camera, Quit. Shown at the default medium scale, where the panel is 480pt wide.

Idle — menu bar icon in the primary label colour, no dot

What each control does

  • Pin — pin.slash off, pin.fill on. Off, the panel closes when another app takes focus, which for an LSUIElement app is the moment anything else is clicked. On, it stays. Clicking the menu bar icon still closes it either way. The choice persists in UserDefaults under panelPinned.
  • Audio toggle — one control, not two. Click to start, click again to stop. The glyph is mic.fill in both states; only the fill changes, to red, while recording.
  • Camera toggle — opens and closes the live preview. It does not change what is recorded; recording is audio either way.
  • Quit — stops an in-progress recording first, then terminates.

The camera picker and the S/M/L buttons are absent in this state. Both appear only while the camera toggle is on.

There is no “Audio” label. It was removed: it named one control in a row of four and left the other three unnamed, so it was reading as a heading for the whole panel rather than as the caption it was meant to be. Every control now depends on its glyph, its tooltip and its VoiceOver label equally.

Two signals change together, deliberately: the symbol goes from slashed to solid and the button fills. Pin is the one control whose effect is invisible until the user clicks somewhere else, so it carries the redundancy the audio toggle no longer does.

The pin is never disabled. The tooltip follows the state — “Keep panel open” when off, “Let panel close on its own” when on — while the VoiceOver label stays “Keep panel open” in both.

The camera toggle is never disabled. Permission problems are reported inside the preview, not by disabling the control that opens it — a disabled toggle would give the user nothing to read.

Empty and first-run

  • There is no empty state for the row: the same four controls are present from first launch.
  • First run has no onboarding. The two Shortcuts named “Start” and “Stop” are a prerequisite the app does not check for; a missing Shortcut surfaces as the error alert on the first Start.
  • First run is unpinned and medium. Neither default is announced anywhere; the pin is discovered by clicking it, and the size buttons are not visible until the camera is turned on.
  • Camera permission is requested the first time the camera toggle is turned on, not at launch. Until it is granted the preview shows its waiting state.

Dark mode

Every surface here is semantic: windowBackgroundColor behind the panel, standard bordered-button chrome for the controls, separatorColor for the constant outline, controlAccentColor for an on toggle. Only the amber menu bar dot is a fixed colour, and it is not on this page. Toggle the switch at the top to check both appearances.
