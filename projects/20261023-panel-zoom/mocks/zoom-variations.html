Panel zoom: replacing S / M / L

Five sizes: Small (400pt), Medium (480pt), Large (half screen), X-Large (three quarters), Full (screen width). Zoom out disables at Small, zoom in disables at Full. Each variation is live: click its buttons. The thumbnail shows the panel on a 1512×982pt MacBook screen (right anchored, as today).

Fit check. The preview is 16:9, so a full-width panel is as tall as width × 9/16 plus the row. On a 16:10 MacBook that fits (1512 wide gives ~886pt tall). On a 16:9 external display (e.g. 2560×1440) full width needs ~1476pt against ~1415pt of visible height, so it overflows; the thumbnail turns red when that happens. Toggle the display below.

A. Two magnifier buttons

Separate bordered capsules with magnifier minus / plus glyphs (SF Symbols minus.magnifyingglass, plus.magnifyingglass). Most self-explanatory glyphs; costs two full control widths plus spacing.

B. Joined minus / plus pair

One bordered capsule split by a hairline, like Preview's and Safari's zoom pair. Narrowest option (about 50pt), reads as a single "size" control. No indication of the current size in the row itself.

C. Pair with size readout

Same joined pair with the current size between the buttons (S, M, ½, ¾, Full). Keeps what the S/M/L letters used to tell you, which a bare pair loses. Widest of the three (about 85pt), so it comes out of the camera menu.
