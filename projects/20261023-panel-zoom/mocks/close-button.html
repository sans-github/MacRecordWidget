Close-panel button (⌘W), left of Quit

All rows are drawn by the mock kit at the real Small (400pt), Medium (480pt) and Large widths. The camera menu is the only flexible item, so the new button comes out of its width: at Small that is about 26pt (the 22pt button box plus spacing), and "Logitech BRIO" truncates further. Tooltip in every variation: "Close panel (⌘W)".

A. Close glyph, standalone

xmark as an off-state capsule at full row spacing before Quit. Plainest reading of "close".

B. Close glyph paired with Quit

Same xmark, held 2pt from Quit like the zoom pair, so the two "leave" actions read as one group. Saves one row gap, so the camera menu loses less at Small.

C. Collapse glyph, paired with Quit

chevron.up: "fold back into the menu bar". Avoids an xmark sitting next to power, which some may read as two ways to quit.
