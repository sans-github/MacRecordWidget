Preview — running while recording

Start has been pressed. Photo Booth is open behind the panel and the Start shortcut has fired. The preview does not stop, pause or restart (AC-PV-5).

Mirror
  • Only the button row changes versus the running state: Start dims, Stop enables. That is the shipped recording behaviour, unchanged.
  • The preview keeps the same session and the same layer. Nothing in the Start or Stop path touches it (AC-PV-5).
  • The picker and the mirror checkbox stay enabled during recording. Switching camera mid-take changes the preview only, and the preview is not being recorded, so nothing is at risk.
  • No recording badge is drawn over the preview image. The recording indicator already exists in two places — the menu bar icon and the enabled Stop button — and a third would be duplication that also implies, falsely, that this image is the one being recorded.
  • The video toggle stays on and stays enabled. Turning it off mid-recording collapses the panel back to 200pt and stops the preview session; it does not stop the recording.

The mismatch this state exists to expose

The indicator here reads Logitech BRIO. Photo Booth may be recording the FaceTime HD Camera; the app cannot read or set Photo Booth's device, so it cannot say. What the label promises is narrow and always true: this is the camera in this image. A user who sees BRIO here and a built-in camera framing in Photo Booth has everything they need to spot the mismatch, without the app claiming knowledge it does not have (AC-PV-6, AC-PV-7).

What is unchanged

No new control, no new modifier, no new accessibility element in this state. The button row mapping is in the master recording mock; the preview mapping is on the running page. This page exists to show that the two compose without either changing.
