requirements:
  summary: Turning on the video toggle opens a live camera preview below the button row, so the user can frame the shot before recording instead of discovering a bad angle in Photo Booth. The preview keeps running while recording is in progress; Photo Booth remains the actual capturer and the preview never writes a file. The user picks which connected camera to preview (Logitech BRIO, built-in FaceTime, or any other), and can flip mirroring on or off; both choices persist across app launches, with mirroring on by default to match Photo Booth. The camera session stops when the popover closes and resumes on reopen, so the camera activity light cycles with the panel. Permission and hardware states are explicit: first toggle-on triggers the system prompt, denied access shows an inline message with a shortcut to the camera privacy settings, and a disconnected or missing camera falls back gracefully without blocking recording.
  key_points:
    - "Preview placement and lifecycle: below the button row, 200pt panel width unchanged. Panel grows taller only while the toggle is on and collapses when off. Session starts on toggle-on, stops on popover close, resumes on reopen. Preview stays live throughout recording."
    - "Camera picker, persisted: user chooses among all connected video devices; selection survives relaunch. If the saved device is unplugged, the preview says so and falls back to another connected camera rather than showing a black rectangle. DECIDED: the picker affects the preview only, not what Photo Booth captures. The two may disagree; the PRD documents this as a known limitation and does not warn the user."
    - "Mirror toggle, persisted: user-facing control, default on. Applies to the preview only."
    - "States to design for: permission not determined (prompt on first toggle-on), denied or restricted (inline message plus an open-settings action), no camera connected, selected camera disconnected mid-session, camera busy. In every failure state the record and stop buttons stay usable; a broken preview must never block recording."
    - "Crowding is a design constraint for the Designer, not prescribed here: the panel is a 200pt row with four controls, and the preview plus camera picker plus mirror toggle roughly doubles the control count. The picker may be better as a menu on the preview area or an overflow control than a fifth item in the row."
  additional_context: |
    Settled with the user before the PRD: preview sits BELOW the button row (not above). Preview keeps running during recording. Session stops when the popover closes; the user accepts the camera activity light cycling. Mirroring is a user-facing toggle, not a fixed default. Camera picker is in scope.
    Engineering prerequisites surfaced during the interview, not yet in scope: NSCameraUsageDescription does not exist in the app's Info.plist today, and persistence of the camera and mirror choices needs a storage location since the app has none yet.
