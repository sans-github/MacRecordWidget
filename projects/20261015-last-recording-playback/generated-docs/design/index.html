Last Recording Playback: Mocks

Draft mocks for playing the last recording inside the panel. Decided: one “play last” control (option B), merged into the timer box, whose glyph says the medium (waveform / play.rectangle.fill); mirror disabled during video playback; video returns to live when it ends; no keyboard shortcuts. Visual baseline and CSS: projects/master/mocks. Not yet approved.

  • The “play last” control: how it shows audio vs video before pressing (recommended glyphs + 2 alternatives), S/M/L widths, width recommendation, Option A appendix
  • Audio states: idle, nothing recorded, opening, playing, finished, can’t find
  • Video states: opening, playing, paused, missing, auto-return to live, mirroring callout
  • Exclusivity: what recording disables, record during playback, state matrix

Glyph decision: no play/stop swap

The play last control keeps its medium glyph (waveform or play.rectangle.fill) and flips its fill, like the record toggles; it never swaps to stop. The row’s rule is “lit means active, press again to end it”; the pin is the only glyph swapper and adding a second one would make the rule two rules. Transport glyphs that do swap (play/pause) live in the video overlay, where QuickTime’s convention applies and users expect it. The VoiceOver label carries the action: “Play your last recording, audio, 04:17” / “Stop playing the last audio”.

Keyboard, menu bar, motion, contrast

Keyboard. No shortcuts, matching the shipped app; the floating transport's own play/pause is click-only. Menu bar. The app is LSUIElement with a MenuBarExtra and has no app menus; the status icon does not change during playback (it reports capturing, and playback is not capturing). Reduce Motion. The progress line and scrubber move linearly with time and carry information, so they stay; nothing new animates decoratively. Increase Contrast. The overlay material gains a 1pt white 50% stroke; white on #007AFF is 4.02:1.

Open: audio pause

Audio playback is play/stop only (the simpler choice); pause is not designed. Flagged for the user.

Feasibility flags for EM (not designed around)

1. Reading the newest .m4a from Voice Memos’ group container may trigger the macOS “would like to access data from other apps” prompt or be refused; the alert above covers refusal but not the prompt’s timing. 2. Identifying “the last” audio file: the Start shortcut passes Recording-<timestamp>, but whether Voice Memos’ filename relates to it is unverified. 3. Whether the camera session keeps running under video playback (privacy light stays on) or pauses; pausing reintroduces the start latency on Live Camera.
